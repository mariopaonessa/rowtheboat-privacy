Row The Boat – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short: Row The Boat does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your race results stay on your device.

Who we are

Row The Boat is a rowing game developed by Mario Paonessa ("we", "us"). This policy applies to the Row The Boat app on iOS, Android and macOS.

Data we collect

None. The app does not connect to the internet or to any server of ours or of third parties. We cannot see who uses the app or how they use it.

Data stored on your device

To show your ghost boat, your top 3 races and your records, the app saves your race results (distance, time, splits, stroke data and date) in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your device settings.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to connect to your rowing machine (for example a Concept2 PM5 or another Bluetooth FTMS rower) and read live workout values such as speed, stroke rate, power and distance. These values are used during the race and for your on-device records only. They are not sent anywhere.

Location permission (older Android versions)

On Android 11 and older, Android requires the location permission for any app that scans for Bluetooth devices. Row The Boat asks for it only for this reason. The app does not read, store or share your location. On Android 12 and newer, the app does not request location access.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell or share data with anyone.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal data, we also collect no data from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the Swiss FADP), you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to provide or delete; all app data is under your control on your device.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
